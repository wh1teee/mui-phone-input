This is the **stable 1.2.1 patch release** of `@wh1teee/mui-phone-input`. It
polishes how the country selector feels.

## Selector motion and icons

- The shadcn popup grows from its trigger (`transform-origin` follows the
  anchor) and fades out faster than it opens.
- The trigger chevron turns while the list is open in MUI and Base UI.
- Close and selected indicators are drawn SVG icons instead of text glyphs.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion`, the fade stays and movement is removed.

JavaScript behavior and declarations are unchanged from 1.2.0.

## Stable 1.0 compatibility retained

The stable 1.0 API remains compatible: React Hook Form (RHF), Zod, server,
locale, flag, and renderer-specific exports keep their contracts; this patch adds no
exports. The supported
metadata preset family remains **max, min, mobile, and validated custom
metadata**. Default paths retain max metadata; `/min` paths retain their
explicit `metadata.min.json` authority and entrypoint bundle budgets.

The main executable closure budget moved to 33,506 bytes gzip (+147 bytes for
selector motion and drawn icons), keeping its 96-byte headroom.
The exact package passes the production Next.js 16 App Router and Vite
consumers without package-specific transpilation or server-externalization.
The verification apps now use Next.js 16.3.8, above the patched floor for
`GHSA-vcvr-r3jv-pc5j`; the published library does not depend on Next.js.

The fresh exact-artifact RideOS validation remains the previously merged RideOS
PR #442 evidence; this release has not been revalidated in RideOS.
Physical-device and assistive-technology evidence remains explicitly
`NOT_AVAILABLE`, as previously owner-accepted and recorded in `mpi-oan.24`.
The deferred Christofle item `mpi-oan.19` remains unchanged.
